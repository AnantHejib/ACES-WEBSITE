What is ACES?

The Association of Computer Engineering Students (ACES) is the official student body representing the Department of Computer Engineering at Sinhgad Institute of Technology (SIT), Lonavala under the esteemed Sinhgad Technical Education Society (STES).

ACES is the vibrant pulse of our department — harmonizing technological excellence with creative celebration. We organize premier national-level hackathons, coding leagues, and technical workshops, alongside high-energy departmental cultural festivals, live music, dance showcases, stage drama, and annual galas.

Whether innovating code for real-world solutions or commanding the center stage, ACES empowers every student with confidence, leadership, and lifelong memories.

💻 Hackathons & Coding 🎭 Cultural Fests & Drama 🚀 Tech Workshops 🎉 Annual Galas

Our Vision

"To emerge as a premier departmental student forum that nurtures technically astute, culturally vibrant, ethical, and inventive computer engineers capable of solving complex global problems while celebrating creative excellence."

Our Mission

  • ✓ Technical Acumen: Provide experiential learning through hands-on workshops on AI, Cloud, Cyber Security, and Full-Stack frameworks.
  • ✓ Cultural & Creative Expression: Celebrate artistic passions through grand cultural fests, live music, dance, theatre, and annual galas that build stage poise, confidence, and camaraderie.
  • ✓ Competitive Spirit: Host national symposiums, algorithmic code clashes, and collaborative 24-hour hackathons.
  • ✓ Industry Bridge: Connect students with tech alumni, corporate recruiters, and open-source project mentors.
  • ✓ Holistic Leadership: Instill professional ethics, effective communication, organizational management, and teamwork.
